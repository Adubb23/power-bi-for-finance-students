# 📊 Power BI & Data Analytics Essentials for Finance & Business Students

A free, self-paced Microsoft Learn roadmap built for finance, accounting, and business students who want to go beyond basic Excel spreadsheets and build interactive, executive-ready financial dashboards—**no prior coding experience required**.

## 🎯 What You Will Learn
* **Clean & Transform Messy Data:** Use Power Query to automate repetitive spreadsheet cleanup and prepare financial datasets.
* **Data Modeling for Business:** Connect multiple tables and build reliable relationships for accurate reporting.
* **Interactive Dashboard Design:** Create clear, dynamic visualizations that highlight KPIs, trends, and financial insights for stakeholders.

## 💡 Why Power BI Matters for Finance & Business Roles
In internships and entry-level analyst interviews, knowing how to turn raw CSVs or Excel exports into automated dashboards sets you apart immediately. Every module in this plan is 100% free, browser-based, and tracked on your Microsoft Learn profile so you can showcase your progress on LinkedIn and your resume.
